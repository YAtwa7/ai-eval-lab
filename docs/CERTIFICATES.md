## How to record one

When a certificate is issued, add a row with:

- the exact credential name as printed on the certificate,
- the issue date,
- the verification URL or credential ID,
- the course/session that produced it (e.g. "S-007"),
- where the artifact is stored (not in the repo if it contains personal data).

## Claiming notes

- **LangChain Academy** completion certificates: the platform ships a
  student-certificate page, so the mechanism exists, but the exact issuance
  text sits behind sign-in. Confirm on the first login and correct
  [COURSE-MAP.md](COURSE-MAP.md) if it differs.
- **DLAI short courses** award a certificate per course, gated on the graded
  assignment. Their long *Professional Certificate* tracks are different -
  auditing is free but the certificate needs payment or financial aid.
- **Claude Academy** issues a "Completion badge". That is a badge, not an
  accredited certificate; describe it as a badge.
- **HF Agents Course** issues a "certificate of completion by completing
  assignments", and separately runs a formal certification track.
- **LCAE** is the only credential here that is a proctored exam with an
  Accredible-issued certificate. It is the one worth the $99 — and only once
  a Ghaia-shaped role is live.
